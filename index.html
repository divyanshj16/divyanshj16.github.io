Brief bio

I am a research engineer working at the intersection of applied machine learning, computer vision, robotics, geospatial AI, and healthcare. I am currently based in New York and working at NYU Langone Health, where I focus on building AI systems for healthcare applications.

I am also pursuing a Master of Science in Computer Science at Georgia Institute of Technology. Previously, I worked as a machine learning engineer at Woven by Toyota and as a data scientist at Esri R&D Center, where I contributed to deep learning capabilities in the learn module of the ArcGIS API for Python.

My research and engineering interests include 3D computer vision, generative modeling, vision-language systems, robotics, and deep learning for GIS and healthcare. I graduated from Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University with a major in Electronics and Communication Engineering in 2019.
